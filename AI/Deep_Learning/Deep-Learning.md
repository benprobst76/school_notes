A set of [[Machine-Learning]] algorithms that model high level abstractions in data by using model architectures that are know as neural networks. Used in natural language  processing, speech recognition, image processing…
- They are based on artificial neurons.  
- They can do both supervised and unsupervised tasks and are deeply connected to many of the ML models.  
- They have achieved impressive performances on many tasks
- Applications: automation for vehicles, surveillance, image understanding, etc.
Gap between human and artificial intelligence: reasoning & inference, causality modelling, model reliability, structure modelling and symbolic components.
### Why Now?
Jürgen Schmidhuber: It is a bit like the last neural network (NN) resurgence in the 1980s and early 1990s, but with million-times-faster computers. Apparently, we will soon  have the raw computational power of a human brain in a  desktop machine. That is more than enough to solve many essential pattern recognition problems.
Richard Sutton has similar remarks on the scalability of algorithms over computing power. And you can also find more remarks from Geoffrey Hinton, Yoshua Bengio, and Yann LeCun.
