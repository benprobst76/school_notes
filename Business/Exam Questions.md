What three lessons from the course do you anticipate will be most useful in your future professional life? Explain why each lesson matters to you and how it connects to your goals or values.
### Answer
#### Context
- Start at IBM after graduation working is an SRE
- I have aspirations to be a tech entrepreneur from a SaaS product
#### Lesson 1
- The Stanley Robotics case provides a great lesson in starting with the problem that customers want solved.
- Don't start with a solution that customers don't need
- Talk to customers before you build and be ready to learn and adjust as you go. 
	**Why:** This matters to me because it fits with my goal of creating a SaaS product that is useful to people. I value solving real problems that people will appreciate. This lesson will be critical for creating a product that customers will want. 
#### Lesson 2
- The teach-in on how to break into an industry dominated by a monopoly 
- Target underserved customer segments that incumbents tend to ignore
- Leverage emerging technology before competitors adapt
- Remove friction points that monopolies have normalized
- Focus on what customers value, not what the market leader offers 
- Build new business models that make the old ones feel obsolete
	**Why:** This matters to me because I value creating a niche product that will be highly valuable to small subset of customers. This lesson will be critical because it highlights how a product can stand out from more general solutions that might have a monopoly. 
#### Lesson 3
- Teach-in on how to advertise
- Star with warm-outreach to gain practice in pitching your product to customers then move on to strategies like cold outreach, free content and paid ads. 
- Use technology and targeting to make ad more effective
	**Why:** This matters to me becasue in order for my SaaS to be successful people need to know about it. This aligns with my goal to create a SaaS that is succsseful enough to replace my day job.
