The Innovator's Dilemma explains how established companies often fail when faced with disruptive technologies because they focus on sustaining innovations that improve existing products for their most profitable customers. These large companies are hesitant to invest in smaller, emerging markets for disruptive technologies because they seem unprofitable and may cannibalize their current business, which gives startups the opportunity to grow and eventually overtake them. The book's solution is for large firms to create small, agile, and independent business units to pursue these disruptive innovations without the constraints of their existing business model.
